PDF page 2: SKILL RELEVANCE LEVELS (numeric conversion continues on page 3)
HIGH = A core, dominant, or strongly required characteristic.
MEDIUM = Frequently relevant but not the main focus.
LOW = Present but secondary or minor.
NOT_RELEVANT = No meaningful evidence.
HIGH = 3; MEDIUM = 2; LOW = 1; NOT_RELEVANT = 0.

Pages 2-3: use the existing six LearnMatch skill domains. Base relevance on the
course description, obtainable skills and career paths. Do not assign HIGH solely
because a course belongs to a cluster. Do not force all six domains to be relevant.

PDF page 3: RIASEC definitions
R - Realistic: hands-on activities, tools, equipment, technical operations,
practical work, building, repairing, operating systems or equipment.
I - Investigative: analysis, research, scientific thinking, problem-solving,
logical investigation, experimentation, and discovery.
A - Artistic: creativity, design, artistic expression, content creation,
creative work, and innovation.
S - Social: helping people, teaching, counseling, communication,
service-oriented work, and working closely with people.
E - Enterprising: leadership, management, persuasion, business,
entrepreneurship, sales, and decision-making.
C - Conventional: organization, documentation, records, procedures,
data management, structured systems, and administrative work.

PDF page 3: RIASEC RELEVANCE LEVELS
HIGH = Dominant or core characteristic.
MEDIUM = Frequently relevant.
LOW = Secondary or minor characteristic.
NOT_RELEVANT = No meaningful evidence.
HIGH = 3; MEDIUM = 2; LOW = 1; NOT_RELEVANT = 0.
Only assign dimensions supported by the provided course data; explain the evidence.

Current implementation note (not an instruction from the PDF): selected skill
domains require weights 1-3; leave unsupported skill domains unselected. RIASEC
requires all six entries, using 0 for no meaningful supporting evidence.
No scoring or validation change is made by this reference.
